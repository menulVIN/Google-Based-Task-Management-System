# OneSapro Design System

The single source of brand, visual, and UI truth for **OneSapro** — the hospitality
technology ecosystem built by **Texonic Information Systems (Pvt) Ltd** (Colombo + Singapore).
OneSapro is not "just a PMS"; it is an ecosystem connecting hotels, restaurants, travel
agents, guides, taxis, suppliers and guests on one cloud platform. Tagline: **"Beyond PMS with AI."**

This design system encodes the **official June 2026 green brand palette** and the
**organic / human-first voice** the social team is moving toward — and gives design agents
the tokens, components, UI kits and slide templates to produce on-brand work.

## CONTENT FUNDAMENTALS — how OneSapro writes

The voice is mid-transition from **corporate B2B** to **organic, human-first** (Brand Voice V2).
When writing copy in this system, follow V2.

**Core theme threading all content:**
> "Every broken hotel experience you've ever had as a guest was a systems failure. Not a people failure."

**Voice principles**
1. **Human before brand** — the first line is never about OneSapro. Never open with the company name or "We are proud to announce…".
2. **Specific before general** — "A hotel in Ella" beats "a hotel in Sri Lanka"; "Tuesday morning" beats "recently"; real numbers beat round ones.
3. **Relatable before technical** — lead with an analogy (brain, traffic light, orchestra, immune system) before PMS jargon.
4. **Earn the product mention** — 80% insight, 20% OneSapro. Links go in the first comment, never the body.
5. **Laugh at the industry** — the hospitality-tech space takes itself too seriously; don't.

**Voice / mechanics**
- **Person:** First person singular for Dr. Chaminda ("My research found…", "I drove 4 hours to Ella…"). First person plural ("we", "our team") for company posts. Address the reader as **you**.
- **Casing:** Sentence case for almost everything. ALL-CAPS reserved for short kicker labels (e.g. `SYSTEMS OVER SYMPTOMS`) and stat callouts. Headlines are Title Case or sentence case — not shouty.
- **Tone:** Direct, confident, no filler. Strong opinions welcomed ("This is wrong, and I'll tell you why"). Warm, not corporate. Sri Lankan pride + global ambition.
- **Numbers:** Always specific. "47 minutes." "53 modules." "200+ OTAs." "Under 1 hour." "3× faster." Specificity = credibility.
- **Vocabulary:** Say **"zero investment"** (never "free tier"/"freemium"). Say **"ecosystem"** not just "software". Name real people (Vishan, Sithara, Nisala) and real hotels (with permission).
- **Emoji:** Used sparingly as section markers in internal/strategy docs (🧠 🚦 🫁). In public-facing posts, prefer words over emoji. Not an emoji-forward brand.

**Headline hook archetypes** (for slides & posts): the Contrarian, the Uncomfortable Truth, the Specific Number, the Personal Story, the Information Gap, the Bold Claim with Proof. Never start a hook with "The", "We", or "Our".

**Examples of on-voice lines**
- "Your brain was built to survive in a jungle. Not manage a 60-room hotel."
- "Colombo's traffic lights coordinate 10,000 cars. Your channel manager coordinates 200 booking platforms. One of these works perfectly."
- "I drove 4 hours to Ella last week. The owner called me at 6am — first time in 5 years he woke up to zero availability conflicts."

## VISUAL FOUNDATIONS

**Color.** A **green ecosystem**, signalling growth, calm and trust — deliberately *not* the
generic B2B blue. Primary Green `#00712D` grounds hero surfaces and dark panels; Lime `#7EC60F`
is the single energetic accent (kicker labels, underlines, key numbers, CTA highlights); Medium
Green `#5FA201` and Deep Forest `#1D5D4B` handle secondary accents and dark dividers/shadows.
Charcoal `#323232` is body text on light; white is text on green. **Max ~3 colours per surface.**
Orange is quarantined to Texonic content only.

**Type.** Geometric and confident. **Montserrat** (bold/extrabold) for headlines, **DM Sans**
for body/UI, **Axiforma SemiBold** as the display/wordmark face. Stat numbers go big and black
(72–96px). Labels are small, uppercase, letter-spaced (`0.12em`). Body sizes never below 14px;
slide body never below 24px.

**Layout & spacing.** Generous breathing room; a 4px-based spacing scale. Content is grid/flex
with explicit `gap`. Compositions favour a strong kicker → headline → supporting-line rhythm,
often with a lime rule or underline anchoring the headline.

**Backgrounds.** Two modes: **light** (white / warm-tinted `--green-wash`) for "Clean Authority"
report-style layouts, and **deep green / dark** (`#00712D`, near-black forest) for "Bold Statement"
hero and stat layouts. **No purple/blue gradients, no busy textures, no generic stock-lobby photos.**
Real photography only — Dr. Chaminda, the team, actual hotels — optionally tinted with a low-opacity
green overlay to tie to brand.

**Corner radii.** Moderate and friendly, echoing the rounded wordmark: 10–16px on cards, 6px on
small controls, pill (`999px`) on tags/labels and primary buttons.

**Cards.** Soft, low-contrast, **green-tinted shadows** (not neutral grey), 1px subtle border
(`--line-200`) optional, 16px radius. They feel premium and calm, not flat-material and not heavily
drop-shadowed.

**Shadows.** Three soft tiers, all tinted with forest-green rgba so elevation feels warm. Focus
state is a 3px lime ring (`--shadow-focus`).

**Borders & dividers.** Hairline `--line-200` on light; on green surfaces, dividers are translucent
lime (`--divider-on-green`).

**Hover / press.** Hover = slight darken of greens (or lift to next shadow tier on cards). Press =
brief shrink (`scale(0.98)`) and/or deeper green. Transitions are short and calm (~150–200ms,
ease-out). **No bounces, no flashy motion** — entrances are gentle fades/rises.

**Transparency & blur.** Used lightly: low-opacity lime washes behind text blocks, translucent
green overlays on photos, translucent lime dividers on dark. Glassmorphism is not a core motif.

**Imagery vibe.** Warm, natural, real. Sri Lankan/Maldivian hospitality settings. Authentic and
slightly lo-fi over polished and staged.

## ICONOGRAPHY

OneSapro has **no proprietary icon font** in the supplied assets. The brand's own iconic element is
the **geometric isometric "OS" monogram** (the logo icon — see `assets/logos/onesapro-icon.png`),
built from stacked lime + charcoal cubes; use it as the brand glyph / favicon / avatar, not as a
general UI icon set.

For UI and slide iconography this system standardises on **[Lucide](https://lucide.dev)** (loaded
from CDN) — a clean, consistent **outline** set with a ~2px stroke that matches the brand's geometric,
uncluttered feel. Use line icons at a single stroke weight; tint them `--green-medium` or charcoal on
light, lime or white on green. Avoid filled/duotone icon styles and avoid emoji in public-facing
visuals. **Substitution flagged:** Lucide is a stand-in for a brand-owned set that doesn't exist yet —
swap it if OneSapro adopts an official icon library.
